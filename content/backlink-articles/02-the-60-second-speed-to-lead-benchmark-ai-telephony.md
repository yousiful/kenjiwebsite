## The Human Limitation in Speed-to-Lead

Why do companies fail at speed-to-lead? The breakdown is structural, not motivational:
1. **SDR Context Switching:** A human Sales Development Representative (SDR) or front-desk receptionist is typically occupied on another call, reviewing paperwork, or on break when a fresh lead enters the system.
2. **After-Hours Coverage Costs:** Staffing a high-skill, 24/7/365 US-based inbound phone desk requires a minimum of 4 full-time employees, costing upwards of $20,000 per month in payroll and benefits.
3. **Offshore Call Center Friction:** Delegating front-line phone coverage to low-cost offshore agencies frequently results in dropped nuances, script rigidity, and disappointed prospects.
