## Why Native AI Injection Beats Third-Party Middleware

Most businesses attempting to build AI call centers make a critical mistake: they string together 4 or 5 separate tools using Zapier, Make, or custom webhooks:
- Form software -> Zapier -> Third-party voice API -> Webhook -> External CRM.

When one connector hiccups, calls drop, latency spikes past 2,500ms, and prospective customers hang up.

**KenjiAI solved this by eliminating middleware completely:**
- **Sub-500ms Latency**: Callers experience near-instant conversational responses with realistic human inflection and speech barge-in (the AI stops speaking immediately if the customer interrupts).
- **Native CRM Memory**: The AI voice agent reads and writes directly to the CRM database in real-time, accessing contact history, notes, and technician availability without lag.
- **STIR/SHAKEN Compliance**: Phone numbers are certified and reputation-managed, ensuring calls display as verified rather than "Spam Likely".
