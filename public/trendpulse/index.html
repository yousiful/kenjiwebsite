Discover What's Spiking Before The Market Catches Up.

TrendPulse and AdForge is a free trend and ad research tool from KenjiAI. It pulls rising searches from Google Trends along with tech and social feeds, then helps you turn a trend into an offer, ad angles and hooks you can test.

What you can do with it

  • Live Trend Radar: rising topics by category (e-commerce, AI and tech, health and wellness, finance), with search volume and the related queries people type.
  • Ad Spy and Longevity: study ads that have run for 90 days or more on Meta, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. An ad that keeps running is usually paying for itself.
  • Executive Dossier: unit economics and buyer psychographics for a niche in one page.
  • $100M Offer Architect: shape a trend into an offer with a clear outcome, bonuses and a guarantee.
  • Ad Angle Matrix and Viral Hook Vault: six angles and short UGC hooks for the same offer, ready to test.
  • Search Intent Miner and Campaign Vault: the questions buyers search before they buy, and a place to save campaigns you build.

Free to use, no signup. More free tools for ads, follow-up and reviews are on the KenjiAI free tools page.
