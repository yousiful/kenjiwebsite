Discover What's Spiking Before The Market Catches Up.

TrendPulse and AdForge is a free trend and ad research tool from KenjiAI. It pulls rising searches from Google Trends along with tech and social feeds, then helps you turn a trend into an offer, ad angles and hooks you can test.

What you can do with it

  • Live Trend Radar: rising topics by category (e-commerce, AI and tech, health and wellness, finance), with search volume and the related queries people type.
  • Ad Spy and Longevity: study ads that have run for 90 days or more on Meta, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. An ad that keeps running is usually paying for itself.
  • Executive Dossier: unit economics and buyer psychographics for a niche in one page.
  • $100M Offer Architect: shape a trend into an offer with a clear outcome, bonuses and a guarantee.
  • Ad Angle Matrix and Viral Hook Vault: six angles and short UGC hooks for the same offer, ready to test.
  • Search Intent Miner and Campaign Vault: the questions buyers search before they buy, and a place to save campaigns you build.

How to use it

  1. Open the Trend Radar and pick a category you sell in, or one you are thinking about.
  2. Look for topics with rising search volume and read the related queries. Those are the words buyers use.
  3. Check Ad Spy for long-running ads in that space to see which offers and angles already work.
  4. Build your offer in the Offer Architect, then write angles and hooks to test. Save the campaign in the Vault.

Questions

Is TrendPulse free?

Yes. TrendPulse and AdForge is free to use with no signup and no card. You open the page and start searching.

Where does the trend data come from?

Rising searches come from Google Trends, along with tech and social feeds. Each trend shows its search volume and the related queries people type, so you can see what is behind the spike.

How do I turn a trend into an ad?

Pick a rising trend, open the Offer Architect to shape it into an offer with a clear outcome, then use the Ad Angle Matrix and Hook Vault to write six angles and short hooks you can test with a small budget.

Why look at ads that have run for 90 days?

Advertisers turn off ads that lose money. An ad that has run for three months or more is usually paying for itself, so it is a good sign of an offer and angle that work.
