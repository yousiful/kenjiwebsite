    Read this before you plan your life around a number

    • This is a calculator, not a promise. It only shows what the math pays if you hit the numbers you typed in. Most people don't hit them, especially early on.
    • The company publishes what it actually paid: in 2025 it paid an average of $11,443 for the whole year to its life insurance-licensed agents in the U.S. and Canada (earnings disclosure). Many agents earn far less, and some earn nothing.
    • You're an independent contractor. Licensing, travel, marketing and other business costs come out of your pocket and aren't in these numbers.
    • Commissions are usually advanced. If a family cancels their policy early, that commission gets charged back.
    • Income comes from helping families get protected. Recruiting by itself pays nothing.
