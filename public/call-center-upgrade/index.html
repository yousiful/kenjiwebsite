Real Questions

Before you ask, here's the honest answer.

Isn't $199-399/mo plus usage a lot for an answering service?

A part-time answering service costs about the same and still puts callers on hold, closes at 5pm, and can't dial a lead back the second they fill out a form. One recovered $7 sale a month already covers the base fee on the metered plan. This isn't priced against "free," it's priced against every lead you're already losing to a call that rang out.

I already have a receptionist or answering service. Why switch?

Keep them for what they're good at. This isn't a replacement for a person, it's coverage for the hours and the leads a person can't get to fast enough, nights, weekends, and the 60-second window after someone submits an ad form. Most clients run it alongside their existing team, not instead of them.

Does it actually sound human, or is it obviously a bot?

The demo video above is the same voice model live on real calls today, not a scripted sample. The Unlimited tier adds voice cloning so it can run in your own brand voice. Most callers don't realize they're talking to AI until you tell them.

What if a caller asks something it can't answer?

It books the appointment, tags the call, and flags it for a human follow-up instead of guessing. You get the transcript and recording either way, so nothing falls through silently.

How fast can this actually be live?

Most accounts are answering real calls within days of onboarding, not weeks. Existing KenjiAI clients move even faster since your CRM connection already exists.

Is there a contract, or can I cancel anytime?

No long-term contract. You can move between the metered and unlimited tier anytime, and cancel the add-on without touching your core KenjiAI account.
