# Homepage (/) surface brief

Scope: kenjiai.com homepage, src/pages/HomePage.tsx. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners doing $10K+/mo arriving from ads, reels and search. Job: understand in seconds that Media Traffics/KenjiAI is an ads agency first and software second, and start the growth quiz.
Proof allowed: 5x return on ad spend, $186K from one funnel, 500+ businesses, running ads since 2013, 5.0 across 31 Google reviews (ReviewsNative quotes). Never $3.35M as a client result. No demos, no call center, no call simulator.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is one argument, the same ad spend without a system vs with ours. It refuses the feature-grid homepage (18 cards, simulators, tool tiles).
OWN-WORLD: The growth quiz's world: #0B0E14 ground with a #142133 top glow, warm #F3EEE6 type, Bricolage Grotesque 800 display, teal #5EEAD4 for "with us" and the action, muted coral #FFB4A1 for "without", hairline white/10 rules, no cards, no gradients on text.
STORY: The visitor sees their own leaky funnel on the left and the fixed one on the right, learns that we run the ads and KenjiAI does the follow-up, sees real results and reviews, then answers question 1 of the quiz.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Short H1 across the top ("Same ad budget. Very different month."), then a two-column ledger, Without a system (coral, strikethrough-ish losses) vs With Media Traffics + KenjiAI (teal gains), rows aligned line by line and ending in the outcome row: money burned vs 5x return on ad spend. Below it, the working quiz Q1 as 4 answer buttons.
FORM: comparison ledger (grounded list #6), seed 76ce509a. Signature interaction: on load the "with" column's rows light up one after another (row by row reveal); reduced motion shows all.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
