## 🔧 **Customization Guide**

### **How to Update Company Name**

Search and replace `KenjiAI` with your company name:
```tsx
// Current
KenjiAI makes no guarantees...

// Change to
[Your Company] makes no guarantees...
```

### **How to Update Specific Claims**

Update revenue claims to match your site:
```tsx
// Current
"$500K+/month" and similar statements

// Change to match your claims
"[Your Specific Claim]" and similar statements
```

### **How to Add/Remove Sections**

Each section is independently contained in a `<div>` block:
```tsx
<div className="max-w-5xl mx-auto mb-6 sm:mb-8">
  <div className="bg-gray-800/50 border border-gray-700 rounded-xl p-4 sm:p-6">
    {/* Section content */}
  </div>
</div>
```

Simply add, remove, or modify entire blocks as needed.

### **How to Update Governing Law**

Update jurisdiction:
```tsx
// Current
...governed by the laws of the United States and the state in which KenjiAI operates...

// Change to
...governed by the laws of [Your Jurisdiction]...
```
