# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of service and local businesses already doing $10K+ a month (contractors, home services, agencies, coaches, financial and tax firms) who want more paying customers from ads and don't have the time or know-how to run ads and follow-up themselves. A secondary audience is newer owners and marketers who aren't ready for done-for-you. They get the $7 Ad Optimization Community.

## Product Purpose
Media Traffics / KenjiAI is an ads agency first and a software company second. The agency side runs paid ads (Meta, Google, YouTube) that win attention. The software side, KenjiAI (built on GoHighLevel), turns that attention into income with fast lead replies, automatic follow-up, a CRM and booking. Success means the client makes more money from every ad dollar.

## Positioning
"We hack attention, then turn it into income." Founder-led, running ads since 2013, with the ads and the follow-up system under one roof, built and set up for the client. AI made easy and simple to use. The pitch is the outcome (more income), not the tech.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from Meta ads, social reels and search. The main conversion path is the growth quiz (kenjiai.com/growth-quiz): qualified owners book a 15-minute walkthrough (GHL calendar), and everyone else is offered the $7 community. Pricing lives at /pricing. Netlify auto-deploys from GitHub main.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Done-for-you ads, KenjiAI CRM/follow-up/booking software, an optional AI call center add-on, and the $7 community.
- The founder doesn't want the AI call center, call simulators or product demos on the homepage (2026-10-08).
- The homepage should be simple: lead with results and the agency-first/software-second story.

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: direct, raw, no fluff, plain human language. No em dashes, AI-sounding phrases, all-caps or forced hashtags (see the no-ai-slop rules).
- Pages must feel warm and serving: no banners, scarcity, exit popups, guilt lines or pulsing CTAs, and low clutter.
- Don't say "$X generated" in ad-style copy. Sell the outcome: better ads plus better follow-up = a 5x return on ad spend.

## Evidence on Hand
- 5x return on ad spend for clients (the founder confirmed 2026-10-08 that he can back it up).
- $186K from a single client funnel.
- 500+ businesses served; running ads since 2013.
- 31 Google reviews averaging 5.0, with real verbatim excerpts in src/components/ReviewsNative.tsx.
- **$3.35M is company revenue, not client results.** Never present it as money clients made.
- Not on hand: client logos, video case studies, named before/after numbers per client. Don't fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles
1. Outcome over features: every section answers "how does this make me money?"
2. Attention first, income second: ads are the hero, the software is the engine behind them.
3. Simple beats complete: fewer sections, one clear next step.
4. Proof must be real and correctly attributed.
